\par\vspace{4pt}\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\scriptsize \emph{Notes:} Panel regressions with month fixed effects; standard errors clustered by firm and month ($t$-statistics in parentheses). The peer return is standardized each month. Card-spending growth is the year-over-year log growth of the firm's share of total panel card spending in month $t$, $t+1$, $t+2$ (none of which is public at the end of month $t$) and their average. Coefficients on the peer return and on the announcement variables are multiplied by 100 for card-spending growth, the forecast error, CAR, and the return, so they are in percentage points; SUE and the revenue surprise are in units of their own standard deviations; the coefficients on own lagged growth and own return are not rescaled. SUE is the seasonal change in EPS scaled by its standard deviation over the previous eight quarters; the forecast error is actual minus the last IBES median forecast, scaled by price (\%); the revenue surprise is the seasonal change in sales scaled by its standard deviation; CAR[$-1,+1$] is the market-adjusted announcement return (\%). Announcement variables refer to the first earnings announcement in months $t+1$ to $t+3$. Controls: own return, own-industry peers' and industry returns at the FF48 and NAICS-4 levels, same-category returns, log size, log book-to-market (set to zero for firms with negative book equity, with an indicator for them), and momentum, all at $t$. *, **, and *** denote significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels.\end{minipage}
