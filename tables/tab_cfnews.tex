The peer return is standardized each month. Card-spend growth is the year-over-year log growth of the firm's share of total panel card spending in month $t$, $t+1$, $t+2$ (none of which is public at the end of month $t$) and their average. Coefficients on the peer return and on the announcement variables are multiplied by 100 for card-spend growth, the forecast error, CAR, and the return, so they are in percentage points; SUE and the revenue surprise are in units of their own standard deviation; the coefficients on own lagged growth and own return are not rescaled.
