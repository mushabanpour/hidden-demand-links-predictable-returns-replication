\par\vspace{4pt}\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\scriptsize \emph{Notes:} The dependent variable is the stock's month-$t+1$ return. Explanatory variables are measured at the end of month $t$. The customer-overlap peer return is the month-$t$ return of other sample firms weighted by the share of customers they have in common with the firm (equation~\eqref{eq:peer}; card data from $t-12$ to $t-1$). Own-industry peers' and same-category returns are leave-one-out averages over sample firms in the firm's FF48 industry and in its SafeGraph category (its most frequent monthly category over months $t-12$ to $t-1$), equal- or value-weighted; the own NAICS-4 peers' return is the equal-weighted average over the other sample firms in the firm's 4-digit NAICS industry (Compustat). The shared-taste return weights peers by RPS (Section~\ref{sec:rps}) and the customer-home return by \textsc{Home}; the store-footprint return weights peers by \textsc{Store}; the TNIC-3 return is the equal-weighted return of TNIC-3 rivals; the FF48 and NAICS-4 industry returns are the value-weighted returns of all CRSP stocks in the firm's industry excluding the firm; the customer return follows \citet{CohenFrazzini2008}; the HQ-city and shared-analyst returns follow \citet{ParsonsSabbatucciTitman2020} and \citet{AliHirshleifer2020}, respectively. When a firm has no link in a network (TNIC-3, HQ city, shared analysts, customers, same category, NAICS-4 industry), that peer return is set to its monthly cross-sectional mean. For firms with negative book equity, log book-to-market is set to zero and the negative-book-equity indicator equals one. Columns (1)--(7): Fama-MacBeth regressions with Newey-West (four lags) $t$-statistics. Columns (8)--(11): pooled OLS with fixed effects and standard errors clustered by firm and month. All regressors are winsorized at 1\% and 99\% each month. *, **, and *** denote significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels.\end{minipage}
