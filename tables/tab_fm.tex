\par\vspace{4pt}\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\scriptsize \emph{Notes:} The dependent variable is the stock's month-$t+1$ return. Explanatory variables are measured at the end of month $t$. The customer-overlap peer return is the month-$t$ return of other sample firms weighted by the share of customers they have in common with the firm (equation~\eqref{eq:peer}; card data from $t-12$ to $t-1$). Own-industry peers' and same-category returns are leave-one-out averages over sample firms in the firm's FF48 industry and in its SafeGraph category (the most frequent category of its stores over months $t-12$ to $t-1$), equal- or value-weighted. The shared-taste return weights peers by RPS (Section~\ref{sec:rps}) and the customer-home return by \textsc{Home}; the store-footprint return weights peers by \textsc{Store}; the TNIC-3 return is the equal-weighted return of TNIC-3 rivals; the industry return is the value-weighted FF48 return of all CRSP stocks excluding the firm; the customer return follows \citet{CohenFrazzini2008}; the HQ-city and shared-analyst returns follow \citet{ParsonsSabbatucciTitman2020} and \citet{AliHirshleifer2020}. When a firm has no link in a network (TNIC-3, HQ city, shared analysts, customers, same category), that peer return is set to its monthly cross-sectional mean. Columns (1)--(7): Fama-MacBeth regressions with Newey-West (four lags) $t$-statistics. Columns (8)--(11): pooled OLS with fixed effects and standard errors clustered by firm and month. All regressors are winsorized at 1\% and 99\% each month. *, **, and *** denote significance at the 10\%, 5\%, and 1\% levels.\end{minipage}
